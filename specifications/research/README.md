## Authority and status

Distillations are research records, not contracts. When a record and the
maintained corpus disagree, the corpus wins. Authoring, metadata, and review
rules live in the
[documentation workflow](../../docs/development/documentation-workflow.md).
The parent [specifications index](../README.md) routes to the contracts these
records inform.
